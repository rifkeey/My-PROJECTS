e-ISTROM: THE ISTROM OFFICE SYSTEM (Version 1)
===============================================

HOW TO OPEN IT
--------------
Double-click "e-Istrom.html" in this folder.
It opens in your web browser. Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge work best.
No internet or installation is needed.

TRYING IT OUT
-------------
The system starts with Istrom's real 15 projects and 745 units (taken
from istromgroup.com.ng). People, clients, payments and money figures are
SAMPLE data so you can see how everything works.

On the sign-in screen, click any role under "Try it as..." to see the
system the way that person would. Every sample account uses the password:
    istrom2026

GOING LIVE WITH REAL DATA
-------------------------
1. Sign in as the Managing Director.
2. Go to Settings > Backup & data > "Clear sample data".
   Projects and units stay. Everything else is removed, and you create
   the first real administrator account.
3. Settings > Company: add the company bank account (it prints on letters).
4. Staff: add each staff member, then use "Sign-in access" to give them a
   role and a temporary password.
5. Settings > Import from Excel: bring in existing clients, buyers and
   balances, staff, vendors and store items.

IMPORTANT: YOUR DATA
--------------------
Everything is saved inside the web browser on THIS computer.
  - Always open e-Istrom with the same browser.
  - Don't clear the browser's history or site data for this file.
  - Make a backup every week: Settings > Backup & data > Download backup.
    Keep the backup file on a flash drive or Google Drive.
  - To move e-Istrom to another computer, copy this whole folder, open it
    there, and use "Restore" with your latest backup.

Version 1 runs on one computer. Putting it online so every staff member can
use it from their own laptop or phone is the next step.
